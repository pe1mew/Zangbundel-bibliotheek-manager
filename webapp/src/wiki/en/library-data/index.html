Manual

Library

The library itself has these details. You fill them in in the left bar, under Library. Fields marked * are required: without them the library cannot be saved.

Title

The library’s name as the app shows it. Required, 1 to 80 characters. If someone already has a library of the same name, the app asks on import whether to replace it.

Description

A short explanation for whoever imports the library: who or what the songs are for. Optional, up to 1,000 characters.

Licence

On what terms others may use and share the library. Required; the app shows it on import. A song with its own licence keeps it; this one applies to the other songs. Choose mixed if every song has its own licence.

SPDX identifier

The SPDX code of another licence, such as MIT or CC-BY-4.0. The list is at spdx.org/licenses.

Licence summary

One sentence that explains the licence, 1 to 200 characters. Required; the app shows it before the full text. For a common licence the manager fills it in, and you may change it.

Full licence text

The full licence text, if you want to include it. Optional, up to 32,000 characters. Without it the app shows only the summary.

Name

Who looks after the library, so that rights holders and singers with a correction know whom to contact. Required. The name is in every copy of the library.

E-mail or website

An e-mail address, or a website starting with https://. Required. Everyone who imports the library sees it; a group address serves as well as a personal one.
