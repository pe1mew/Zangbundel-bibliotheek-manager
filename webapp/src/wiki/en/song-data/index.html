Song details

Every song has these details. You fill them in in the left bar, under Song details, while you edit the song. Fields marked * are required. Press F1 in a field, or click ⓘ, for that field’s explanation.

Title

The song’s name as singers know it. Required. Keep it short: up to 60 characters fits every screen. If the library has more songs with this title, give them another language or a subtitle (in the ChordPro text).

Language

The language of the words. Required. For a song in two languages: the language of the first verse. Not in the list? Choose “Other language code…” and type a language code, such as fy or nl-BE.

Words

Who wrote the words. Separate several names with ;. Unknown or handed down? Type unknown or traditional.

Music

Who wrote the melody. Separate several names with ;. For a folk tune, type traditional.

Copyright

Who holds the rights, for example 2026 Zangbundel project, or Public domain if the song is free. The app puts © in front.

Licence

On what terms the song may be shared: an SPDX code such as CC-BY-SA-4.0 or CC0-1.0, or public-domain, or proprietary. Left empty, the library’s licence applies.

Source

Where the words come from: a book, a website, or handed down in your group, so that someone else can check it later.

Tags

Keywords to filter on, such as kampvuur, welpen or kerst. Separate them with commas. Lower-case letters, digits and hyphens only.

Song ID

The song’s fixed identifier. It is proposed from the title when the song is applied, and never changes after that, not even when the title does: setlists in the app refer to it.
