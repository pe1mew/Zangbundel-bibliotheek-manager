Zangbundel library manager

Making song libraries for the Zangbundel app

Being built

This is where the library manager will be: a website on which scout leaders without technical knowledge make, check, update and sign their own song library for the Zangbundel app. Your songs and your key stay on your own computer; nothing of them goes to this server.

The manager is being built and tested now.
