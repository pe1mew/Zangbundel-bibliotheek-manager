## 1. Open or start {#start}

Choose **Open a library** and pick a `.zbl` file, or choose **New library** and give it a title. The manager checks an opened library straight away.

## 2. The library's details {#library}

In the left bar, under **Library**, fill in the title, the licence with a short summary, and the maintainer's name and e-mail address or website. See [Library](../library-data/).

## 3. Songs {#songs}

**Add a song** opens the editor. You edit the song in **Blocks** (verse, chorus, bridge) or as **Code (ChordPro)**, and fill in the [song details](../song-data/) on the left. **Apply** puts the song in the library. **Import song** adds a `.cho` file.

## 4. Sets {#sets}

Under **Sets**, **New set** makes a setlist. Add songs with + in the **Songs** list. Move a set by dragging its handle, or with Alt+↑ and Alt+↓.

## 5. Check {#check}

**Check** in the bottom bar shows errors and warnings. **Go to** takes you to where you fix it. With an error the library cannot be saved; a warning is advice.

## 6. Download {#download}

**Download** saves the library as one file, with a new version number. Share that file any way you like.

## 7. Signing (if you like) {#sign}

A key signs the library, so the app shows that it comes from you. Make a key once with **Make key** and keep the key file safe, out of shared folders. Load it later with **Load key**; **Download** then signs the library. Never send your key file to anyone. The manager forgets the key with **Forget key**, after 30 minutes without use, and when you close the page.

## 8. In the app {#app}

Import the file in the Zangbundel app. The app checks it with the same rules and shows whether it is signed, and by whom.
