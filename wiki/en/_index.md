title: "Manual"
---

With the **Zangbundel library manager** you create, check and edit song libraries for the Zangbundel app, and sign them if you like. A library is one ZIP file: you share it any way you like, by mail, chat, a website or a USB stick, and the app imports it.

Everything happens in your own browser. Your songs and your key do not go to the server, and nothing is kept when you close the page. Only https://zangbundel.nl is the library manager.

This manual is available in Dutch, English and German.
